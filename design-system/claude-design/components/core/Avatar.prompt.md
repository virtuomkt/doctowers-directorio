Circular doctor photo with white ring border; falls back to initials on a light teal tile when no photo is set. `AvatarGroup` overlaps up to `max` avatars, used in the "+200 especialistas confían en DocTowers" trust strip.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Ana Ruiz" src="/doctors/ana.jpg" size={40} />
<AvatarGroup avatars={[{name:"Ana Ruiz"},{name:"Luis Vega"},{name:"Marco Díaz"}]} max={4} />
```
